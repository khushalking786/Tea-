Proper Whole-Leaf Storage Guidelines

To maintain full flavor and floral aroma, store whole tea leaves in an airtight, opaque container away from heat, direct light, humidity, and strong spices. Best kept in a dry pantry between 18°C and 24°C. Never refrigerate opened packs as condensation damages the tea leaves.
